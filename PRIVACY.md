# Privacy policy

*Effective 15 October 2026.*

GlideGrail is a set of ServiceNow coding standards: a markdown document, an
agent skill that points to it, and Open WebUI add-ons that load it. It
collects no personal data, and nothing you do with it reaches the author.

## What it does

- **The document and skill** are text. Your AI assistant reads them on your
  machine or in your assistant's own service. GlideGrail sends nothing
  anywhere.
- **The Open WebUI filter and tool** run on your own Open WebUI server. They
  download the latest `GlideGrail.md` from `raw.githubusercontent.com` about
  once a day. That request carries no information about you or your chats.
  If it fails, or you turn it off, they use a copy built into the add-on. Your
  messages are read on your server only to choose which sections to add, and
  are never sent anywhere.

## Third parties

Downloading GlideGrail, or installing it from a directory such as GitHub,
the Claude plugin directory, skills.sh, Smithery, cursor.directory or the
Open WebUI community hub, is governed by that service's own privacy policy.
So is the AI assistant you use it with.
